Privacy Policy

Lakelow: Water Drainer
Effective Date: July 28, 2026
Last Updated: July 28, 2026

🛡️ In Short

We never ask for your name, email, or phone number. The game does show ads and uses analytics, and those services collect limited device data — including your advertising ID. This page explains exactly what is collected and how to turn it off.

Introduction

FluxiPlay ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Lakelow: Water Drainer mobile game application ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect information that directly identifies you. However, the advertising and analytics services built into the Game automatically collect limited technical and device information.

Collected through Unity Analytics

  • Anonymous gameplay events (session start, session end, level progress)
  • Device model, operating system version, and language
  • Game performance and crash data
  • A randomly generated app instance identifier

Collected through our advertising partners

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, Identifier for Advertisers on iOS)
  • IP address, and the approximate region derived from it (country/city level — not precise GPS location)
  • Device type, operating system, screen size, network type, and language
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, clicked, or completed

We do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any account or login credentials

How We Use This Information

  • To operate the Game and keep it stable across different devices
  • To understand how players progress so we can balance and improve levels
  • To fix crashes and performance problems
  • To display advertising, measure it, and prevent ad fraud

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use this data to build a personal profile of you outside the Game.

Advertising

Lakelow: Water Drainer is free to play and is supported by advertising. We use Unity LevelPlay as our ad mediation platform, which serves ads from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads.

These partners may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to measure ad performance. They act as independent data controllers for the data they collect, and their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

If you opt out of ad personalization on your device (see Your Choices below), you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized — chosen based on general context rather than your advertising identifier.

Third-Party Services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) from the following providers. Each has its own privacy policy governing the data it collects:

Unity Technologies

Provides Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform (including ironSource Ads, which is part of Unity).

Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
ironSource Privacy Policy: is.com/privacy-policy

The Game is distributed through app stores such as Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those platforms collect their own data about downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Your Choices

You can limit ad tracking at any time directly from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track

You can also stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the Game.

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the data associated with you, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California may also request that their personal information not be sold or shared.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a similar jurisdiction, the Game will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can request that we help you exercise any of these rights by emailing games@fluxiplay.com with your advertising identifier so we can pass the request to the relevant partner.

Data Retention

We retain anonymous analytics data only as long as it is useful for improving the Game. Data held by our advertising and analytics partners is retained according to their own policies, which are linked above.

Data Security

We rely on established third-party providers who use industry-standard safeguards to protect the data they process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. By playing the Game, you consent to this transfer.

Children's Privacy

Lakelow: Water Drainer is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
